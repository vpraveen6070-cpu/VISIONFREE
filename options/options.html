Section D — Medical Disclaimer, Privacy & Research Notice

Professional Prescription Notice: Please enter values obtained from a certified eye-care professional. VISIONFREE does not perform eyesight tests, diagnose eye health, or determine prescriptions.

Experimental Status: Prescription values collected in this phase are stored solely for research and future processing pipelines. The current version does not perform optical blur correction, wavefront deconvolution, or replace prescribed spectacles.

Privacy & Local Storage: All profile and prescription data are stored exclusively inside your local Chrome profile using chrome.storage.local. No data is transmitted to external servers, cloud databases, or third-party analytics. Please note that local browser storage is not an encrypted medical health-record vault.

      Digital Typography & Human Vision

      Human visual perception relies on subtle contrast transitions and comfortable spatial frequencies. When reading long articles on digital displays, text layout directly influences cognitive stamina. Optimal paragraph line spacing prevents the reader from losing their place between lines, while subtle letter tracking minimizes visual crowding. These adjustments allow our visual cortex to recognize character wordforms more fluidly. By tuning digital display parameters to individual viewing comfort, readers can engage with technical and literary content with greater ease, reducing the fatigue often associated with prolonged screen sessions.
